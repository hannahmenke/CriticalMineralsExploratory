# Resources Policy Submission Readiness Checklist

Based on the current Resources Policy Guide for Authors, the main submission-facing requirements are:

- Article type: submit as a Perspective.
- Length: target 4,000-6,000 words for the manuscript word count, excluding references.
- Abstract: maximum 250 words; the current abstract has been revised without citations.
- Keywords: 1-7 keywords; seven are now included in the manuscript.
- Highlights: submit a separate highlights file with 3-5 bullets, each under 85 characters.
- Review model: double-anonymized peer review, so prepare a title page and an anonymized manuscript for submission.
- CRediT: author contribution roles are required; the current manuscript includes a CRediT-style statement.
- Declarations: complete Elsevier's competing-interest declaration tool and upload the generated Word file.
- Funding: confirm whether to add a funding statement or state that no specific funding supported the work.
- Generative AI: confirm the exact disclosure wording for AI-assisted drafting/editing before submission.

Remaining editorial tasks before submission:

- Keep the manuscript within the 4,000-6,000 word Perspective range, excluding references.
- Decide whether to keep both figures and the large facility table in the submitted version.
- Prepare anonymized and non-anonymized manuscript files.
- Confirm all author affiliations, contributions, competing-interest declarations, funding statements, and AI-use declaration with coauthors.
