# Journal Decision Table

Here is a decision table for the current draft, including `InterPore Journal`.

| Journal | Fit to current draft | Rewrite needed | Best audience | Main advantages | Main risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Resources Policy` | `Strongest overall` | `Low to moderate` | Critical minerals policy, government, industry, resource strategy | Best match to your current `policy + infrastructure + strategic minerals` framing; audience already spans academia, government, and industry | Could still feel a bit method-heavy unless DRP is consistently tied to policy and investment outcomes |
| `InterPore Journal` | `Strong scientifically, mixed strategically` | `Moderate` | Porous media, DRP, imaging, reactive transport community | Very natural home for `digital rock physics`, `imaging`, `flow-through systems`, `DLE sorbents`, `reactive transport`, `engineered porous materials`; strong community alignment | Audience is narrower and more method-centered; less direct reach into critical-minerals policy circles; newer journal with less established signaling power |
| `Minerals Engineering` | `Strong technical fit` | `Moderate` | Mineral processing, extractive metallurgy, geometallurgy, process mineralogy | Good fit for `comminution`, `liberation`, `heap leaching`, `DLE`, `recycling`, `AI/digital twins`, and processing optimization | Current manuscript is more strategic/perspective-driven than a typical mineral-processing paper; may need more technical sharpening and less policy framing |
| `Hydrometallurgy` | `Targeted but narrower` | `Moderate to substantial` | Hydrometallurgy, leaching, SX/IX, aqueous processing | Strong fit for `heap/in-situ leaching`, `DLE`, `effluent analysis`, `reactive flow cells`, `solution processing` | Too narrow for the full manuscript as written; exploration, broader DRP infrastructure, and policy framing may feel out of place unless the paper is re-centered on aqueous extraction |
| `Environmental Science & Policy` | `Plausible but not natural` | `Substantial` | Environmental policy, science-policy, sustainability governance | Sustainability and legitimacy framing could work here, especially with UK/EU emphasis and public decision-making | Would need a real reframing away from `DRP as technical infrastructure` toward `environmental knowledge, governance, and decision support`; otherwise it may read too sector-specific |
| `The Extractive Industries and Society` | `Weak to moderate` | `Substantial` | Extractives governance, social impacts, development, society | Could work if you foreground `social licence`, `public legitimacy`, and environmental accountability in extractives | Current paper is too imaging/process/infrastructure focused; journal center of gravity is more social-science and governance than your draft |

## Recommendation

If you want the least rewriting and the strongest match to the manuscript as it now exists:

1. `Resources Policy`
2. `InterPore Journal`
3. `Minerals Engineering`

## Simple Decision Rule

- Choose `Resources Policy` if you want maximum fit to the paper’s current `strategic/policy` identity.
- Choose `InterPore Journal` if you want to speak most directly to the `DRP and porous media community`.
- Choose `Minerals Engineering` if you want to reposition the paper toward `mineral processing and extractive technology`.
